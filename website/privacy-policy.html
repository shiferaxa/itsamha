Privacy Policy

AE Autofill Tool Chrome Extension

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the AE Autofill Tool Chrome Extension ("the Extension", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Chrome extension.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

The Extension allows you to store the following personal information locally on your device:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Physical Address
  • LinkedIn Profile URL
  • Portfolio/Website URL

2.2 Usage Information

The Extension may collect limited usage information to improve functionality:

  • Websites where the extension is activated
  • Form fields that are detected and filled
  • Extension settings and preferences

3. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Automatically filling job application forms on websites you visit
  • Storing your preferences and settings within the extension
  • Providing notifications about detected job application forms

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All your personal information is stored locally on your device using Chrome's secure storage API. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your computer
  • No information is transmitted to external servers
  • Your data is encrypted using Chrome's built-in security measures
  • Only you have access to your stored information

4.2 Security Measures

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Use of Chrome's secure storage APIs
  • No external data transmission
  • Minimal permissions requested from Chrome
  • Regular security updates and improvements

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your personal information to any third parties.

Since all data is stored locally on your device, there is no data transmission to external servers or services. The Extension operates entirely within your browser environment.

6. Permissions

The Extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab: To access and fill forms on the current tab you're viewing
  • storage: To save your personal information locally on your device
  • scripting: To inject form-filling functionality into web pages
  • host_permissions: To work on job application websites

7. Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all stored information through the extension settings
  • Modify: Update or change your information at any time
  • Delete: Remove all stored data by clearing extension settings
  • Disable: Turn off the extension or uninstall it completely

8. Data Retention

Your data is retained locally on your device until you:

  • Manually delete it through the extension settings
  • Clear your browser data
  • Uninstall the extension

9. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the Extension, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the bottom of this policy
  • Notify users through the extension if there are significant changes
  • Continue to protect your data according to the updated policy

11. Third-Party Websites

The Extension works on third-party job application websites. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any websites you visit.

12. Technical Information

The Extension is built using:

  • Chrome Extension Manifest V3
  • Chrome Storage API for local data storage
  • Content scripts for form detection and filling
  • No external APIs or services
